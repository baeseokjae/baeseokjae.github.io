That is the measurement in this article, scoped to what was actually run. On a 12-fact synthetic fixture with two local Ollama embedders, the prohibition “Deployment to production on Fridays is prohibited for all teams.” scored 0.660 against “May I deploy to production on Friday?” under all-minilm:latest. The two descriptive facts in the same fixture scored 0.764 and 0.773 for their own questions. A 0.7 gate therefore keeps the descriptions and deletes the rule, and the ordering reverses on the second embedder — the same prohibition scores 0.949 under snowflake-arctic-embed:33m. Or you can register a tool and let the model call it, with the tool’s result being the JSON you wanted. The practical failure comes from doing both at once, which is a natural thing to do: keep the tools registered, and add format when a structured summary is due.
