Production Debugging Agents: What “Read-Only” Really Protects

An agent that can look at a production incident is useful. An agent that can also change production is a second incident waiting for a bad day. Most teams resolve that tension by choosing one extreme: hand the agent the same credentials an on-call engineer uses, or keep it out of the incident entirely and go back to kubectl and grep. The direct answer to the middle path is this: “read-only” is not one control, it is four, and they do not fail together. The agent’s tool selection is advisory. The other three — the credential or API scope the call travels over, the database transaction mode, and the runtime guard on evaluated expressions — are enforced somewhere outside the agent’s goodwill.
